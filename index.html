01 — The Evolution

It started with a story.

In 2014, Humans of Bombay began with a simple belief: stories can change the way people see the world. Twelve years later, that belief has become a company.

Over the next 12 years, we told more than 7,000 stories — stories of ambition, love, loss, resilience, failure, success and everything in between. Along the way, those stories built a community of over 7 million people and gave us something far more valuable than reach: an understanding of people.

We learned what makes people stop. What makes them feel. What makes them share. What makes an idea stay with them.

And then brands started coming to us. What began as a storytelling platform evolved into a business helping brands create stories, build communities, develop original IPs and reach audiences at scale.

Today, that business has a name: The Human Company.

Karishma Mehta

Founder & CEO, The Human Company

In 2014, Karishma started Humans of Bombay with a camera, a curiosity about people and a belief in the power of stories. Over the past decade she has built one of India's most influential storytelling platforms, growing a community of more than 7 million people.

Under her leadership, Humans of Bombay has helped raise nearly ₹20 crore for individuals and social causes. A TEDx speaker, author and Forbes-featured entrepreneur, she is now building the next chapter through The Human Company.

Powerful stories don't just inspire. They create change.
